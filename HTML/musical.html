What is this festival?

This part of our festival is meant for all music lovers visiting. In this wing, you will be able to enjoy live music from bands both local and abroad, both amateur and professional, both niche and world-famous. You can even sign up yourself with your friends to play! Some of the music that will be played includes:

THE MANLIEST, MOST METAL MUSIC!!

Folkore music!

Singles!

And, possibly, yourself!
