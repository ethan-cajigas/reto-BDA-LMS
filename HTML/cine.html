Film Festival

What is this festival?

This is the film festival that is part of the Chop Chop convention. Here visitors can watch never before seen movies and short-films. You'll have the chance to experience works from all around the world.

This is the perfect chance to enjoy some films you probably wouldn't know of otherwise, and for a very small price. We'll also hold several competitions regarding short-films for different categories, each with their own winner and prize (more info here). Everyone is welcome to participate, or just to share or enjoy our common love for cinema.
