Gastronomy Festival

What is this festival?

This is the gastronomy festival that is part of the Chop Chop Convention. Here visitors have the opportunity to try all kinds of foods and treats! Here you will find many stands where many people from differnt places come to share their delicous meals.

Here is the perfect place to try differnt foods of all kinds with a very small entrance fee. We will also hold several competitions for differnt categories, each with their own winner and prize (more info here). Everyone is welcome here, either to share, participate in the contest or just enjoying the many foods.


Main events

  • Food stalls with meals from different places
  • Dessert stalls with treats from different places
  • Food competitions with prizes
  • Cooking clases with profesional chefs
  • Informational chats with different guest chefs
