Gastronomy Festival

This is the gastronomy festival part of the Chop Chop Convention. Here visitors have the opportunity to try all kinds of foods and treats! Here you will find many stands where many people from different places come to share their delicous meals.

Here is the perfect place to try different foods of all kinds with a very small entrance fee. We will also hold several competitions for different categories, each with their own winner and prize. Everyone is welcome here, either to share, participate in the contests or just enjoying the many foods available.
